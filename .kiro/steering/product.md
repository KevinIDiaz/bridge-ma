# Product: Bridge MA

Bridge MA helps a Massachusetts resident turn a crisis into a sourced sequence of next steps across exactly three programs: SNAP, RAFT, and utility shutoff protection.

Demo persona (fictional): Spanish-speaking Boston single parent, recently lost a job, received a fictional Notice to Quit.

Primary journey (the only approved scope):
1. Describe the situation in natural language or voice; the assistant proposes structured facts.
2. Review and edit the complete fact summary once.
3. View three prioritized next actions, reusable documents, human help, official sources, and a printable bilingual handoff.

Each action card must show: why it appeared, matching status, missing facts, official source + review status, deterministic result vs. generated explanation (visibly labeled), next action, required/reusable evidence, deadline only when safely supported, official portal or human handoff.

Out of scope until approved in docs/decisions.md: arbitrary OCR, database, accounts, equity dashboard, offline mode, shared packets, other programs, or direct agency submission. Teammate playbook content is reference only, not approved scope.
